Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Last updated October 13, 2026

Virtuosity is an iPad application published by Luke Morgan (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. A confirmed account is required before the app can be used.

What Virtuosity does

Virtuosity visualizes MIDI from a digital piano as a piano roll, records performances as Standard MIDI files on the device, and can play them back to the instrument. Optional export creates a package of MIDI, a short metadata text file, and locally rendered audio. You can also keep scratchpads on the iPad and import piano MusicXML.

Account

Sign-in is provided by Supabase Auth. You create an account with an email address and a password. The app will not open your library until that email is confirmed. A reset link can be sent to the same address. Supabase stores the password as a one-way hash. The app does not keep a copy of the password. You can sign out from profile settings.

The account record holds the email address and the sign-in session. The session is stored in the iPad Keychain.

What stays on the iPad

  • Recordings. Note, timing, velocity, and pedal messages from a connected instrument are used to draw the roll. If you record, they are stored as .mid files plus a small JSON sidecar (display name, date, comments, folder, tempo, meter, device name) in the app’s Documents folder. Recordings are not uploaded.
  • Scratchpads and the activity log. Drawings, scratchpad pages, and the on-device practice log stay on the iPad. They are not uploaded.
  • Preferences. Appearance, metronome, and other settings for the pianist on this iPad stay on the device.

Sheet music

The app includes a catalog of public-domain piano scores: title, composer, period, form, key, catalog number, license, and the address of the file. The music itself is not stored in the app or in our database. Searching that catalog does not download a score and does not send your account, name, or recordings.

When you import one piece from the catalog, the app downloads that one public file from the host that publishes it. After the import succeeds, Virtuosity saves the score on the iPad and stores a copy with the signed-in account. If the account copy cannot be saved, the score remains on the iPad.

You can also import a .musicxml, .xml, or .mxl file from the Files app. That file is saved on the iPad and in your private account. It is not added to the public catalog, and other people cannot browse it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you start tone training from the controller menu. That training listens while the piano plays so the app can learn how each note’s tone changes with velocity. The app does not turn the microphone on for ordinary practice, recording, or sheet music.

Sharing

If you use the system share sheet to export MIDI, a practice package, or another file, it leaves the app only through Apple’s share interface, to the destination you pick. We do not receive a copy of that export.

Advertising and analytics

The iPad app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. This marketing site counts page views with GoatCounter. That counter is not part of the app.

Children

Virtuosity is a practice tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. An account uses the email address supplied at sign-up. Recordings and scratchpads stay on the iPad unless you export them. A MusicXML score is stored with the account only after someone imports it.
