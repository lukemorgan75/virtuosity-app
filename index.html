The piano is a percussion instrument, let it speak

A tool for dynamics, articulation, and phrasing

Attention to right notes, right rhythm, and right technique are often a cognitive burden sufficient to yield dynamics and phrasing a bridge to be crossed later. And the aural acuity of these, a skill itself. Virtuosity is made to give a hand-up in these challenges.

Much like sheet music, the piano roll view is an intuitive pitch versus time display. But conversely, Virtuosity does not tell you what or how to play; it only shows what you did, as soon as it happens, preserving the record for your convenient inspection. With intuitive colors and visual elements on the roll, our software paints the full, honest picture of your expression.

Two modes of use

Metanome

Made for the shelf, just like a metronome. Open it and start playing. You can let this run for hours so it is ready whenever you need it. Easily hide/unhide the display at your discretion. Pause, rewind, listen, watch, review. Save snapshots and analyze them right away, or keep playing and come back to it later.

Studio

Record something in the rotation. Review a recording yourself or with your teacher, draw, markup, and add notes, all in an organized file manager. We don’t collect your data, so everything you record and write stays on your device unless you export and share.
