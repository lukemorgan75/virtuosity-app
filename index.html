Become a beta tester

  1. On your iPad, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite on that iPad: testflight.apple.com/join/QHLOJXM9
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Virtuosity runs in landscape.
  4. Connect a digital piano with a USB or MIDI cable, then open the app.

If the invite says the beta is not taking new testers, email lukemorgan750@gmail.com and ask to be added.
