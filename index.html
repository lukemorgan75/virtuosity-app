The piano is a percussion instrument, let it speak

A tool for dynamics, articulation, and phrasing

Virtuosity turns a digital piano into a living score of your practice — every note, its duration and velocity, paint the honest picture of your dynamics and phrasing. Record in real-time, listen back identically with MIDI, this time empowered with visual analytics.

Virtuosity is not a new way of learning piano, it’s a tool like a metronome.

At first glance it may look like another “falling notes” app. Instead, this piano roll view is an intuitive pitch versus time display of what YOU play, with colors showing velocity and other visual elements conveying your full expression.

You don’t have to wait until you can play something at speed to start considering dynamics. Virtuosity makes it easy to see what’s happening.

Two modes of use

Metanome Live

Made for the shelf, just like a metronome. Open it and start playing. You can let this run for hours so it is ready whenever you need it. Easily hide/unhide the display at your discretion. Pause, rewind, listen, watch, review. Save snapshots and analyze them right away, or keep playing and come back to it later.

Studio

Record something in the rotation. Review a recording yourself or with your teacher, draw, markup, and add notes, all in an organized file manager. We don’t collect this data, so everything you record and write stays on your device unless you export and share.
