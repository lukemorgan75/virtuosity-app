About

Built for the bench,
not the studio rack.

Virtuosity is an iPad companion for classical piano students. Connect a digital piano over MIDI — especially a Yamaha P-125 or similar — and watch an 88-key piano roll paint what you play: pitch, length, and how hard each note was struck.

It is not a lesson app and not a DAW. The job is simple: give you an honest picture of phrasing and dynamics so you can hear with your eyes what the ear is still learning.

Record a take, loop a bar, zoom in on a passage, mark comments, keep folders of practice. Playback returns the performance to the instrument. Everything stays on your iPad.
